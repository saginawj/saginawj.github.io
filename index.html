01 BRIEFING

Curiosity is the operating system.

My life-long passion is travel. I’ve been to 175 countries and eventually want to visit all of them. It’s a deliberately slow-burn objective, with a time horizon of “before I die.”

I started taking computers apart as a kid to see how the pieces fit together. I never really stopped. Since then I’ve built platforms across cloud, blockchain, AI, and whatever useful edge of technology appeared next. Right now, that edge is LLMs.
